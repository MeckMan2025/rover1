3. app/nav.py (new) — GpsWaypointNav: 10 Hz pure-pursuit follower over a
   field_data path CSV. Heading comes from GPS course-over-ground (rover
   drives forward-only, vy=0 always, so course == body heading), dead-
   reckoned between 2 Hz fixes from commanded yaw. Phases: acquiring ->
   aligning (straight creep until the receiver reports confident course) ->
   tracking -> done. Gates: hAcc <= 0.5 m required (RTK-grade), start must
   be within 3 m of the path start, pause-on-RTK-loss (motors zeroed,
   resumes; faults after 30 s mid-run / 180 s initial), cross-track abort
   at 2 m, no-progress abort at 20 s, mission cap 300 s. All drive writes
   go through MotorTarget.set_drive(source="follow") so the E-stop latch
   and manual-hold override behave exactly like camera follow.

4. app/main.py — POST /api/nav/start {"path": rel} (default = the sidewalk
   path), POST /api/nav/stop, GET /api/nav/status; nav_* merged into
   /telemetry. Mutual exclusion both ways with BoxFollower (both write
   source="follow"); WS estop also stops a running mission.
